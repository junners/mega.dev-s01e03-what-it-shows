The queued parser fix closes the first problem only. The other two keep coming in after it ships.

1 · The parser reads day-first dates month first

Every tenant here writes the day first. The shared parser reads month first, so day and month swap: “7 April went out on 4 July”. This is the bug #4471 names, and the fix that is queued.

2 · booking-v3 shows a month-first hint

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People do what the hint says and type the month first, then the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The swap looks the same as problem 1, so the subject lines match and decision 0001 merged it. It only comes in through the form, only for the booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it: the form needs a day-first hint for day-first tenants.

3 · Invites one day off

These dates do not swap: they move by one day. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk read these as one-day moves (desk notes) but filed them under the parser by tenant. A one-day move with the right day and month points at time zones, not date order.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line. All three problems read “wrong day” in the subject.
  • Intake tags by the reporter’s browser locale. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and form, so the tags in the table disagree with the problems in both directions.
  • The export pages overlap: some reports come twice. Count by id, once.
