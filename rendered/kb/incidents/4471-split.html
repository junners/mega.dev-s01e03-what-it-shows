1 · The parser reads day-first dates month first

Every tenant here writes the day first. The shared parser reads month first, so day and month swap: “7 April went out on 4 July”. This is the bug #4471 names. Fixed on 5 October: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

2 · booking-v3 shows a month-first hint

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People do what the hint says and type the month first, then the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The swap looks the same as problem 1, so the subject lines match and decision 0001 merged it. It only comes in through the form, only for the booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so the wrong hint goes with it.

3 · Invites one day off

These dates do not swap: they move by one day. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk read these as one-day moves (desk notes) but filed them under the parser by tenant. They stay counted there, as the desk counts them, and they have a ticket of their own. A one-day move with the right day and month points at time zones, not date order. Open: the engineering update that closed #4471 does not cover it, and neither fix changes it.

  • Intake tags by the reporter’s browser locale. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and form, so the tags in the table disagree with the problems in both directions.
  • The export pages overlap: some reports come twice. Count by id, once.
